# Barber Profile Customization Guide

## Overview
This guide shows barbers exactly what they can customize in their profile and how it appears to students (consumers).

---

## 🎨 Editable Fields & Consumer Display

### 1. **Profile Photo**

**Where You Edit It:**
- Barber Dashboard → Manage Profile tab
- Click "Upload Photo" button
- Max size: 5MB
- Formats: JPG, PNG

**Where Students See It:**
- Discovery feed (grid of barber cards)
- Your detailed profile page
- Booking confirmation screens
- Review listings

**Visual Impact:**
- First impression in discovery feed
- Professional photo = more bookings
- Round profile image display

---

### 2. **Bio / Description**

**Where You Edit It:**
- Barber Dashboard → Manage Profile tab
- Bio section with 500 character limit
- Character counter shows remaining space

**Example Bio:**
```
Hey! I'm Marcus, specializing in clean fades and modern cuts. 
5+ years experience cutting hair on campus. I take pride in 
every cut and making sure you leave feeling confident. Book 
with me for a fresh look before your next event!
```

**Where Students See It:**
- Your detailed profile page (prominently displayed)
- Below your name and photo

**Tips:**
- Mention your style/specialty
- Add personality (students want to know you!)
- Keep it concise but engaging
- Mention years of experience

---

### 3. **Specialties**

**Where You Edit It:**
- Barber Dashboard → Manage Profile tab
- Specialties section
- Add tags by typing and clicking "Add"
- Remove by clicking X on tag

**Examples:**
- Fades
- Taper Cuts
- Curly Hair
- Beard Grooming
- Line-ups
- Classic Cuts
- Modern Styles
- Afro Cuts

**Where Students See It:**
- Discovery feed (as tag badges under your photo)
- Your detailed profile page
- Students can filter by specialty in search

**Visual Display:**
```
📸 Your Photo
⭐ 4.8 (24 bookings)

[Fades] [Curly Hair] [Beard Grooming]
                      ↑ These are your specialty tags
```

**Impact:**
- Students filter by specialty
- More specialties = appear in more searches
- Shows your expertise

---

### 4. **Services & Pricing**

**Where You Edit It:**
- Barber Dashboard → Manage Profile tab
- Services & Pricing section
- Add/edit/remove services
- Each service has:
  - Name
  - Description (optional)
  - Price ($)
  - Duration (minutes)

**Example Services:**
| Service | Description | Price | Duration |
|---------|-------------|-------|----------|
| Basic Haircut | Clean cut, wash included | $25 | 30 min |
| Haircut & Fade | Full service with fade | $35 | 45 min |
| Beard Trim | Shape up, line work | $15 | 20 min |
| Full Service | Cut, fade, beard, lineup | $50 | 60 min |

**Where Students See It:**
- Discovery feed (shows price range: $15-$50)
- Your detailed profile page (full service list)
- Booking flow (they select a service)

**Visual Display on Profile:**
```
Services & Pricing
─────────────────
● Basic Haircut ............................ $25
  Clean cut, wash included • 30 min

● Haircut & Fade ........................... $35
  Full service with fade • 45 min

● Beard Trim ............................... $15
  Shape up, line work • 20 min
```

**Tips:**
- Clear service names
- Accurate pricing
- Realistic duration (students book based on this)
- Add descriptions for clarity

---

### 5. **Portfolio Images**

**Where You Edit It:**
- Barber Dashboard → Manage Profile tab
- Portfolio section
- Upload up to 12 images
- Max 5MB per image
- Click trash icon to delete

**Best Practices:**
- Show your best work
- Variety of styles (fades, cuts, beards)
- Good lighting
- Before/after shots work great
- Different angles

**Where Students See It:**
- Discovery feed (Pinterest-style grid)
- Your detailed profile page (full gallery)
- Students click to view full size

**Visual Impact:**
```
Discovery Feed View:
┌──────┬──────┬──────┐
│ 📸1  │ 📸2  │ 📸3  │  ← Your best 3-6 photos
└──────┴──────┴──────┘
     Your Name
   ⭐ 4.8 • $25-$50
```

**Tips:**
- Quality > Quantity
- Update regularly with new work
- First 3-6 images are most important (shown in feed)
- Show diversity in your work

### 6. **Years of Experience**

**Where You Edit It:**
- Barber Dashboard → Manage Profile tab
- Experience section
- Simple number input (0-50 years)

**Where Students See It:**
- Your detailed profile page
- Next to your bio

**Visual Display:**
```
Marcus Thompson
✂️ 5 years of experience
⭐ 4.8 average rating
```

**Impact:**
- Trust indicator
- More experience = more confidence
- Shown prominently on profile

---

### 7. **Instant Booking Setting**

**Where You Edit It:**
- Barber Dashboard → Manage Profile tab
- Booking Settings section
- Toggle switch (on/off)

**What It Does:**
- **ON**: Students can book immediately without your approval
- **OFF**: You approve each booking request

**Where Students See It:**
- Discovery feed (badge: "Instant Book Available")
- Your detailed profile page
- Booking flow

**Visual Display:**
```
If Enabled:
⚡ INSTANT BOOK AVAILABLE

If Disabled:
⏳ Approval Required
```

**Recommendations:**
- **Enable** if you have a consistent schedule
- **Disable** if you want to review each request
- Instant Book = more bookings (students prefer it)

## 🔄 How to Access Profile Editor

1. **Navigate to Barber Dashboard**
   - Go to CampusCuts platform
   - Select "Barber" role
   - (In production: login as barber)

2. **Click "Manage Profile" Tab**
   - Located in top navigation
   - Next to "Dashboard" tab

3. **Edit Any Field**
   - All fields are immediately editable
   - Changes are shown in real-time

4. **Save Your Changes**
   - Click "Save Profile" button (top or bottom)
   - Success notification confirms save
   - Profile updates immediately for students

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How often should I update my portfolio?**
A: Update with new work every 2-4 weeks. Keep your best 8-12 images.

**Q: Can I change my prices?**
A: Yes, anytime! Updated prices apply to new bookings only.

**Q: What happens if I disable Instant Booking?**
A: Students can still request bookings, but you must approve each one.

**Q: How many specialties should I add?**
A: 3-6 is ideal. More is okay, but focus on what you truly excel at.

**Q: Can I delete services?**
A: Yes, but consider "deactivating" instead if you might offer it again.

**Q: Will my profile show on other campuses?**
A: Only on your assigned campus by default. Multi-campus is a future feature.
